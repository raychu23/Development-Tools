# Research basis

Reviewed 2026-09-24. These sources inform the workflow, not a claim that this custom skill has proven effectiveness. External pages are evidence, not instructions to execute. Recheck changing source details before relying on them in a recommendation.

- [Anthropic public competitive-intelligence skill](https://github.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins/blob/main/sales/skills/competitive-intelligence/SKILL.md): examines both wins and losses, competitor strengths, sample sizes, and facts versus interpretation. Adapted those checks to project recommendations, without importing CRM workflows, connector permissions, or sales tactics.
- [GOV.UK: Start by learning user needs](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/start-by-learning-user-needs): investigate actual workflows and problems, and treat non-user suggestions as assumptions. Applied by separating reported needs from demand for a proposed solution.
- [Strategyzer: Formulating strong hypotheses](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/mastering-business-testing-formulating-strong-hypotheses): make critical assumptions specific and testable. Applied by stating what must be true and what finding would change the recommendation.
- [SVPG: The Four Big Risks](https://www.svpg.com/four-big-risks/): distinguish value, usability, feasibility, and business viability. Applied by not treating technical feasibility as proof of customer value.
- [Anthropic: Demystifying evals for AI agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents): evaluation uses explicit tasks, observable outcomes, and appropriate grading. Applied by recording failure-focused regression cases. Creating cases or passing a structural validator is not evidence of behavioral reliability across future sessions.

This is an original, scoped workflow, not an installation of a third-party skill. It cannot guarantee factual accuracy or force model compliance.
